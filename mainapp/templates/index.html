WELCOME TO NOU

About NOU e-Gyan Portal

Nalanda Open University welcomes its students, faculty members and study centres to NOU e-Gyan, an online learning portal designed to support digital education.

The portal provides access to study materials and academic resources. It supports learning, assessment, progress monitoring and communication among students, teachers, study centre administrators and university administrators.
